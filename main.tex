    \section{Research Interest}
     Neuro-Symbolic AI; Multi-Agent Systems; Multilingual Low-Resource NLP (African Languages); Efficient and Controllable Language Generation.

    \vspace{0.2 cm}
    
    \begin{onecolentry}
         I investigate neuro-symbolic and multi-agent approaches to NLP in low-resource contexts. My goal is to develop systems that utilize structured linguistic knowledge to facilitate efficient, reliable, and controllable language understanding and generation, with a focus on African languages.
    \end{onecolentry}
